---
name: Public substantiation rule
description: Publishing standard for customer references, performance claims, timing, availability, and third-party branding.
---

Public customer names, logos, testimonials, portfolio references, quantified outcomes, delivery/response timelines, availability commitments, and third-party logos require written support before publication. Without that support, use anonymized illustrative copy, remove the claim, or make the statement explicitly dependent on the written project scope.

**Why:** The repository did not contain approval or substantiation evidence for prior public social proof, performance figures, timing promises, or external brand use.

**How to apply:** Before publishing or restoring one of these items, retain evidence that supports the exact statement, placement, attribution, and duration. Keep third-party tool references text-only with no partnership or endorsement implication unless brand-use permission has been verified.

**Documented exception:** On August 29, 2026, the project owner confirmed ownership of the portfolio website screenshots and approved restoring them. They also approved displaying the tool/platform logos as identification alongside the existing no-partnership/endorsement disclaimer.
