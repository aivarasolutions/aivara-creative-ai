# Public Media Rights Register

**Reviewed:** 2026-08-26  
**Scope:** website, portfolio, music service page, email nurture templates, tool badges, social links, Open Graph metadata, and public static assets.

## Publication rule

No asset is published unless this register contains its source, rights holder, written license or release reference, permitted channels, required credit, approver, and any expiry or revocation terms. An upload, a local copy, an anonymized caption, or a public third-party embed is not rights evidence.

## External-platform review

The following official SoundCloud resources were checked on 2026-08-26:

- [Embedding a track or playlist](https://help.soundcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/115003453587-Embedding-a-track-or-playlist)
- [HTML5 embedded player](https://help.soundcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/115003449627-The-HTML5-embedded-player)
- [Buttons & Logos / attribution](https://developers.soundcloud.com/docs/api/buttons-logos.html)
- [SoundCloud Terms of Use](https://pages.soundcloud.com/geo/uk_us_ie/legal/terms-of-use/09-2025.std.html)
- [SoundCloud Privacy Policy](https://pages.soundcloud.com/geo/uk_us_ie/legal/privacy-policy.html)

SoundCloud offers an embeddable sharing player, but that does not grant Aivara rights in a recording, composition, artwork, client name, or uploader account. Since no authorization record was available, removing the embeds avoids third-party content delivery, player tracking, attribution mismatch, and misleading anonymization.

## Restoration checklist

Before any removed item is restored, retain and reference here:

1. Creator/rightsholder name and source or original project record.
2. Written license, assignment, release, or statement of work covering the exact asset.
3. Permitted public channels, territory, duration, attribution, and revocation terms.
4. Client portfolio-display approval and redaction review for any customer work, screenshot, trademark, personal data, or testimonial.
5. For audio: master, composition/sync, performer, sample/loop, artwork, and public-web/embed rights; plus proof that the embedding account is authorized.
6. For external embeds: current platform terms review, required attribution/linking, privacy disclosure or consent treatment where applicable, and verification that the item remains public and embeddable.
7. For social links: confirmation that the displayed URL is the organization’s official account and that Aivara controls it.

The approver must record the approval date and re-review date here before publication.
